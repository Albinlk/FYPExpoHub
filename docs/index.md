# FYP Expo Hub Handbook

How to use the FSKM UiTM Jasin Final Year Project platform. Pick the guide
for what you do.

<div class="grid cards" markdown>

-   **Visiting the exhibition**

    ---

    Find projects, booths, the schedule and award winners, and browse past
    projects for ideas.

    [Visitor guide](guides/visitor.md)

-   **Student (CSP600 / CSP650)**

    ---

    Request a supervisor, log progress, submit forms and reports, and see
    your presentations and marks.

    [Student guide](guides/student.md)

-   **Lecturer**

    ---

    Supervisor, co-supervisor, examiner, CSP600/650 lecturer or programme
    head, plus marking booth visits on exhibition day.

    [Lecturer guide](guides/lecturer.md)

-   **FYP coordinator**

    ---

    Semesters, people and roles, assignments, nominations, rubrics, marks,
    presentations and publishing to the exhibition.

    [Coordinator guide](guides/coordinator.md)

-   **Exhibition admin**

    ---

    Event details, the master Excel import, booths, schedule, awards,
    lecturer assignments, visits and settings.

    [Exhibition admin guide](guides/exhibition-admin.md)

-   **Developer**

    ---

    Stack, repository map and how to run, change and deploy the app.

    [Developer overview](developer/overview.md)

</div>

## The two sites

| Site | Address | What's there |
|---|---|---|
| Exhibition | [fskmjasinfypexhibition.site](https://fskmjasinfypexhibition.site/) | Public exhibition, Past Projects Guide, lecturer visits, admin |
| FYP Workspace | [fyp.fskmjasinfypexhibition.site](https://fyp.fskmjasinfypexhibition.site/) | CSP600 and CSP650 for students and staff (sign-in needed) |

One account works on both: sign in on either and you're signed in on the
other. See [Accounts and sign-in](guides/accounts.md).
